Three tools that keep the model on its side of the wall.

Built in Los Angeles, two of them at Kowo Research. Each one puts something deliberate between a person and a machine that would otherwise see too much.

Typer

Swift · macOS 14 · MIT · alpha

On-device autocomplete. A faint suggestion appears at your caret in almost any text field and streams in word by word. It runs on llama.cpp against a small local model, so the wall is the edge of your Mac and nothing crosses it.

typr.frgmt.xyz

Beckett

TypeScript · MIT · Kowo Research

A Discord-native AI engineer. Mention it and it sizes the job itself, then opens a numbered task and hands the branches to a pool of coding agents working in isolated worktrees. One agent is the face and does all the talking. A queue and a worker pool are the hands.

Read the repo

Jingle

Rust · Apache 2.0 · Kowo Research

A password manager for AI agents, built so the agent never sees a password. Secrets leave through exactly four doors: a child process, the clipboard for thirty seconds, a TOTP code, or a print you ask for by name. Everything else comes back redacted.
